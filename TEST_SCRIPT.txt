================================================================
  TESLA RACK TEST -- LIVE NARRATION SCRIPT
  Jacked-up bench test, BENCH_MODE = False
================================================================

Read this aloud as you test. One line at a time. Wait for the
condition described before moving to the next line.

Two people: OPERATOR (at the laptop) and SPOTTER (at the wheel).

================================================================
  PHASE 1: SAFETY CHECK BEFORE ANYTHING ELECTRICAL
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Confirming car is in Park."
SPOTTER:  "Confirmed Park."

OPERATOR: "Confirming parking brake is on."
SPOTTER:  "Confirmed parking brake on."

OPERATOR: "Confirming front wheels are off the ground and free
           to spin."
SPOTTER:  Spins each front wheel by hand.
SPOTTER:  "Both wheels spin freely. Confirmed off ground."

OPERATOR: "Rear wheels chocked?"
SPOTTER:  "Chocked."

OPERATOR: "Anyone or anything within 3 feet of the front wheels?"
SPOTTER:  Walks around the front of the car.
SPOTTER:  "Clear."

================================================================
  PHASE 2: BRING UP THE BUS
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Putting Tesla in Drive Ready. Foot on brake."
        (Press brake, key fob in pocket, dashboard reads 'Drive
         Ready' or equivalent.)

OPERATOR: "Plugging SYS TEC into laptop USB."
OPERATOR: "Plugging SYS TEC into Tesla OBD-II port."

OPERATOR: "Opening sniffer first to confirm bus."
        Run:  python can_sniffer.py
        Press Enter at the prompt.

OPERATOR: Wait for "TESLA CHASSIS CAN CONFIRMED" in green.
        Wait for "0x370 EPAS_sysStatus" line in the table.

OPERATOR: "Sniffer confirms chassis CAN. Asking spotter to verify
           angle decode."

SPOTTER:  Turns steering wheel SLIGHTLY left, then SLIGHTLY right.

OPERATOR: Watches "measured ang" in the sniffer's bottom panel.
        Should change as the wheel moves.

OPERATOR: "Measured angle tracks wheel. Bus is good. Closing
           sniffer."

OPERATOR: Press Ctrl-C to stop the sniffer.

================================================================
  PHASE 3: PASSIVE LISTEN ON THE FULL TEST PROGRAM
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Launching test GUI. Will NOT engage yet."
        Run:  python tesla_steering_test.py

OPERATOR: "Clicking CONNECT."
        (Clicks CONNECT button.)

OPERATOR: Watch top right of GUI.
        Should turn from red "DISCONNECTED" to yellow
        "WAITING FOR 0x370" then green "EPAS LINK OK".

OPERATOR: "EPAS LINK OK confirmed. RX counter climbing."
        (Confirm RX number > 0 and rising.)

OPERATOR: "EAC Status reads INHIBITED. That's expected before
           engaging. Last Error reads NONE."

OPERATOR: "Bus Errors reads 0. Good."

================================================================
  PHASE 4: ENGAGE AT ZERO ANGLE (LOW STAKES TEST)
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Going to engage at 0 degrees. No motion expected --
           the rack is already centered. We're just testing
           that the rack accepts our control message."

SPOTTER:  Stands clear of the front wheels. Watches the wheel
        from the side.

OPERATOR: "Hand on E-STOP button. Clicking ENGAGE in 3, 2, 1."

OPERATOR: Click ENGAGE.

OPERATOR: Watches EAC Status.
        Should transition: INHIBITED -> AVAILABLE -> ACTIVE
        within 1-2 seconds.

CASE A:  EAC Status goes ACTIVE (green). Wheels stay still.
        OPERATOR: "EAC ACTIVE. No motion. System engaged.
                   This is correct."

CASE B:  EAC Status flickers AVAILABLE/INHIBITED.
        OPERATOR: "Flickering. Disengaging."
        OPERATOR: Click DISENGAGE or hit ESC.
        OPERATOR: Take a screenshot of the status panel and
                  text Derek before continuing.

CASE C:  EAC Status reads FAULT.
        OPERATOR: "Fault. Last Error reads [READ THE ERROR]."
        OPERATOR: Click DISENGAGE.
        OPERATOR: Text Derek the error code.

OPERATOR: If Case A: "Holding for 5 seconds."
        Wait 5 seconds with EAC steady at ACTIVE.

OPERATOR: "All good. Going to disengage and try a small angle."
OPERATOR: Click DISENGAGE.

================================================================
  PHASE 5: FIRST MOTION TEST -- +5 DEGREES
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Setting target to +5 degrees. Will engage. Wheel
           should turn slowly to +5."

SPOTTER:  Watches wheel from the side. Keeps fingers OUT of
        the spokes.

OPERATOR: Move slider to +5.0 OR type 5 in the angle box and
        press SET.

OPERATOR: "Hand on E-STOP. Clicking ENGAGE in 3, 2, 1."
OPERATOR: Click ENGAGE.

OPERATOR: Watch the GUI:
        - EAC Status should be ACTIVE (green).
        - Commanded angle should ramp from 0 to +5.
        - Measured angle should follow within ~1 degree.

SPOTTER:  Reports wheel direction.
        "Wheel turning [LEFT/RIGHT]. Stopped at about [X]
         degrees."

OPERATOR: "Note the sign convention. Positive = [LEFT/RIGHT
           per spotter]. Writing this down."

(WRITE DOWN: "+ degrees = wheel turns ____.")

OPERATOR: "Holding for 3 seconds. Then back to zero."

OPERATOR: Move slider to 0.

OPERATOR: Watches wheel return to center.

================================================================
  PHASE 6: TEST THE OTHER DIRECTION -- -5 DEGREES
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Going to -5 degrees. Wheel should turn the OPPOSITE
           way."

OPERATOR: Slider to -5.

SPOTTER:  Reports.
        "Wheel turning [opposite direction]. Stopped at about
         [-X] degrees."

OPERATOR: "Sign convention confirmed."

OPERATOR: Slider back to 0.

================================================================
  PHASE 7: BIGGER ANGLES
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Going to +30 degrees. Bigger swing. Watching for
           any jerking or fault."

OPERATOR: Slider to +30. (The script's internal rate limiter
        will ramp at 50 deg/sec, so it takes about 0.6 seconds
        to get there.)

SPOTTER:  Watches motion. "Smooth?" or "Jerky?"

OPERATOR: If smooth: continue.
        If jerky or sounds bad: hit E-STOP, text Derek.

OPERATOR: After 3 seconds at +30, slide to -30.

OPERATOR: After 3 seconds at -30, slide to 0.

OPERATOR: "Test complete. Disengaging."
OPERATOR: Click DISENGAGE.
OPERATOR: Click DISCONNECT.

================================================================
  PHASE 8: TEST THE FAILSAFES
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Now testing the safety net. Reconnecting."
OPERATOR: Click CONNECT, wait for EPAS LINK OK.
OPERATOR: Click ENGAGE.

OPERATOR: "Slider to +20. Then I'm hitting ESC."
OPERATOR: Slide to +20.
OPERATOR: While wheel is mid-turn, press ESC on the keyboard.

OPERATOR: Should see EAC drop to INHIBITED instantly.
        Wheel should stop where it is. Status bar should
        read "E-STOP: ESC key".

OPERATOR: "ESC failsafe works. Disconnecting to clear."
OPERATOR: Click DISCONNECT.
OPERATOR: Click CONNECT, wait for EPAS LINK OK.
OPERATOR: Click ENGAGE.

OPERATOR: "Now testing the angle clamp."
OPERATOR: Type 100 in the angle box. Press SET.

OPERATOR: Should see "REFUSED: 100.0 exceeds +/- 90" in the
        log.
OPERATOR: "Angle clamp works."

OPERATOR: Slider to 0. Click DISENGAGE. Click DISCONNECT.

================================================================
  PHASE 9: SHUTDOWN
================================================================

OPERATOR: "Closing GUI."
OPERATOR: Close the window.

OPERATOR: "Unplug SYS TEC from OBD-II."
OPERATOR: Unplugs.

OPERATOR: "Tesla off."
SPOTTER:  Puts car back in Park if it isn't, removes foot from
        brake, lets car shut down.

OPERATOR: "Test complete. Writing notes."
